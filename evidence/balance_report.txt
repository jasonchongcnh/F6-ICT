Balance simulations: 1000
extractive : neglect=200
restorative: faithful=11, restoration=189
community  : neglect=10, restoration=190
balanced   : faithful=7, restoration=193
random     : faithful=115, neglect=16, profit=3, restoration=49, solidarity=17

Interpretation
--------------
Each named strategy was tested on 200 deterministic seeds. Pure extraction always
failed through neglect. Specialised restorative, community-focused, and fixed
balanced policies most often produced a restoration ending, but none guaranteed
the highest "faithful" ending. The varied random policy reached all five endings,
showing that seeds and individual event/action combinations materially affect the
result. A single repeated extractive action is not a winning strategy, and the
faithful ending depends on maintaining all three values plus food and water.

Limitations
-----------
These are scripted policies, not real players. Classroom testing is still needed
to measure choice clarity, perceived difficulty, enjoyment, and whether students
discover an unintended dominant sequence. Raw per-run results are retained in
balance_results.csv for independent checking.
